Lenovo Type-C port dead after a recent update? Don’t rush to the service center just yet. 🛠️💻

My USB-C port stopped transferring power and data completely after updating my laptop. Before paying for repairs, I tried this quick BIOS reset fix:

Boot into BIOS.

Select Disable Built-in Battery (your laptop will shut down).

Wait 2–3 minutes, plug in your charger, and power it back on.

Port revived instantly—zero hardware repair needed. ⚡
